Our goat milk bars are made in small batches from a ready goat milk base, finished by hand with herbs and essential oils. They contain no SLS, no parabens and no synthetic fragrance.

## Who may prefer it

- People who enjoy a creamy, soft lather.
- Dry and sensitive skin types looking for a gentle wash.
- Anyone who finds glycerine soap too light.

## The goat milk bars

[Neem Tulsi](/soaps/neem-tulsi-goat-milk-soap), [Honey Oats](/soaps/honey-oats-goat-milk-soap), [Ginger Rosemary](/soaps/ginger-rosemary-goat-milk-soap), [Pomegranate](/soaps/pomegranate-goat-milk-soap) and [Orange](/soaps/orange-goat-milk-soap).

## How it compares

Goat milk sits between glycerine, which is the lightest, and shea butter, which is the richest. If you avoid goat milk, the [Kesar Haldi bar](/soaps/kesar-haldi-papaya-cucumber-soap) is made on a papaya cucumber base without it.
