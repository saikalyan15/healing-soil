Sodium lauryl sulfate is a surfactant used in some cleansing products to make fast, dense foam. Sodium laureth sulfate, or SLES, is a separate ingredient that appears under its own name on a label.

Healing Soil bars are made without SLS and SLES, without parabens, and without synthetic fragrance. Scent comes from essential oils and herbs.

## How our bars feel

The base decides most of it. Glycerine bars feel light and rinse easily. Goat milk bars have a creamier lather. Shea butter bars feel richer and more conditioning. The papaya cucumber base is a goat-milk-free option.

Several of the herbs, neem and tulsi among them, are grown on our farm. Each [soap page](/soaps) says which ingredients are grown by us and which are sourced.

## What to compare on a label

- **Surfactants.** Check SLS and SLES separately.
- **Fragrance.** Check whether the scent comes from essential oils, from synthetic fragrance, or whether there is none.
- **Soap base.** Choose glycerine, goat milk, papaya cucumber or shea butter by the lather and after-wash feel you prefer.
