## What we make

Healing Soil makes handmade soap, plus a lip balm and a body butter when the fruit is in season. The soap is made two ways. Most bars start from a ready glycerine, goat milk, papaya cucumber or shea butter base, which we melt and finish by hand. Some bars are cold process, made from scratch from oils and lye. Each [soap page](/soaps) says which method a bar uses.

## What is grown here and what is bought

Neem, tulsi, lemongrass and marigold are grown on our farm in South Goa. The kokum for our lip balm grows here too. Honey comes from suppliers in Goa. Saffron comes from Kashmir, and turmeric, ginger, rosemary, rose and pomegranate come from Indian farms. Shea butter is sourced from West Africa. The ready bases come from a specialist maker.

We tell you which is which, because the label should answer that question.

## What we leave out

No SLS, no parabens and no synthetic fragrance. Scent comes from essential oils and herbs, and the essential oils change from batch to batch. We can leave them out on request. The full ingredient list is on every label.

## Made to order

We do not keep a shelf of old stock. Every order is made after you place it, in a small batch, and wrapped in paper. That is why we ask you to order by message: we confirm the soaps and the date with you first.
