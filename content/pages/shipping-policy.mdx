## Where we ship

We ship across India from our workshop in South Goa. Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,000 and above. Below that, shipping is ₹100, or ₹150 for the North India states.

## Dispatch and delivery time

Every order is made after you place it. We dispatch within 2 business days of confirming your order. After dispatch, delivery usually takes 4 to 7 business days depending on your location. Metro cities are usually on the faster end, and remote areas may take a little longer.

Larger orders, such as soap squares for events, take longer to make. We confirm the date with you before you pay.

## Courier and tracking

We ship by India Post Speed Post and other courier partners, depending on your location. Once your order is dispatched, we send you the tracking details on WhatsApp.

## Delivery delays

Weather, regional holidays or courier disruptions can delay delivery beyond our usual estimates. We will tell you if we know of a delay affecting your order.

## Incorrect address or failed delivery

Please check your delivery address when you confirm your order. If a delivery fails because of an incorrect address, or because no one was available to receive it, we will work with you to arrange redelivery, which may involve an extra shipping charge.
