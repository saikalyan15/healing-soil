Our glycerine bars are made in small batches from a ready glycerine base, finished by hand with herbs and essential oils. They contain no SLS, no parabens and no synthetic fragrance.

## Who may prefer it

- People who like a light, clean-rinsing lather.
- Oily and combination skin types.
- Warm, humid weather, when a lighter bar feels better.
- Anyone who wants a simple everyday bar.

## The glycerine bars

[Neem Tulsi](/soaps/neem-tulsi-glycerin-soap), [Honey Oats](/soaps/honey-oats-glycerin-soap), [Ginger Rosemary](/soaps/ginger-rosemary-glycerin-soap), [Pomegranate](/soaps/pomegranate-glycerin-soap), [Orange](/soaps/orange-glycerin-soap), [Marigold](/soaps/marigold-soap) and [Loofah](/soaps/loofah-soaps).

## How it compares

Glycerine is the lightest of our bases. Goat milk gives a creamier lather, and shea butter feels richer still. There is no single best base. The useful difference is how each one feels in your routine.

## Use and storage

Wet the bar, work up a light lather with your hands, and rinse. Keep it on a draining dish and let it dry between uses, so it lasts longer.
