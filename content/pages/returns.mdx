## Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp at [+91 74831 00651](https://wa.me/917483100651) as soon as possible with your order details, and we will confirm the cancellation and refund any payment already made. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled. See Returns below.

## Returns

Because every Healing Soil soap is made to order from fresh ingredients, we cannot accept returns or exchanges on opened or used bars, or for a change of mind.

## Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp at [+91 74831 00651](https://wa.me/917483100651) or by email at [hello@healingsoil.in](mailto:hello@healingsoil.in). We will replace or refund at our discretion.

Natural variation in colour, texture, shape and marbling is not a defect. It is part of handmade soap.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the account you paid from, usually by UPI, within 5 to 7 business days. If that is not possible, we will agree the best way to return your payment when we approve the refund.
