Healing Soil bars are made in small batches without SLS, parabens or synthetic fragrance. Goat milk gives a creamy lather, shea butter feels richer, and glycerine is the lightest option. If you avoid goat milk, the Kesar Haldi bar uses a papaya cucumber base.

## What to look for

- **No SLS.** Avoid synthetic foaming agents if that is your preference.
- **No synthetic fragrance.** Scent comes only from essential oils and herbs, and we can leave the essential oils out on request.
- **A short, readable label.** Every ingredient is listed on the label.

## How we make it

Every bar is made after you order, in small batches, so what you receive was made for your order and not held in stock. Some herbs, neem and tulsi among them, are grown on our farm in South Goa. Each [soap page](/soaps) says which ingredients are grown by us and which are sourced.

Always check the label, and patch test if your skin is sensitive.
