faqs:
  - q: "What do you leave out of your soaps?"
    a: "Our soaps contain no SLS, no parabens and no synthetic fragrance. Each soap page lists the ingredients in that bar, and the full list is on the label."
  - q: "How do you make your soap?"
    a: "Two ways. Most bars start from a ready glycerine, goat milk, papaya cucumber or shea butter base that we melt and finish by hand with herbs, essential oils and vitamin E. Some bars are cold process, made from scratch from oils and lye. Each soap page says which."
  - q: "What is the difference between glycerine, goat milk and shea butter soap?"
    a: "Glycerine has a light, clean lather. Goat milk is softer and creamier. Shea butter feels the richest. Choose by the lather and texture you prefer."
  - q: "I avoid goat milk. What can I choose?"
    a: "Our Kesar Haldi bar is made on a papaya cucumber base without goat milk. Our glycerine and shea butter bars are other options. Message us and we will suggest one."
  - q: "Can I have a soap without essential oils?"
    a: "Yes. The essential oils change from batch to batch, and we can leave them out on request. Tell us when you message."
  - q: "How do I order?"
    a: "Message us on WhatsApp with the soaps you would like. We confirm the price and the date, you pay by UPI, and we make and ship your order."
  - q: "Do you ship across India?"
    a: "Yes. Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,000 and above. Below that it is ₹100, or ₹150 for the North India states. Details are in our shipping policy."
  - q: "How long does it take?"
    a: "Every order is made after you place it. We dispatch within 2 business days of confirming your order, and delivery usually takes 4 to 7 business days."
  - q: "Are the soaps suitable for sensitive skin types?"
    a: "Our range includes gentle bars suitable for sensitive skin types. They contain no SLS, parabens or synthetic fragrance. Check the ingredient list, and patch test any new product first."
  - q: "Do you sell to shops, hotels and for events?"
    a: "Yes, as 50g soap squares from 25 pieces per soap. See the business page, then message us for the price."
  - q: "Is each bar made to order?"
    a: "Yes. We do not keep a shelf of old stock, so each bar is made after you order."
