## Who we are

Healing Soil is a small-batch handmade soap maker based on a farm in South Goa, India. You can browse our soaps on this website at [healingsoil.in](/) and place orders by messaging us on WhatsApp. If you have any questions about this policy, write to [healingsoil.in@gmail.com](mailto:healingsoil.in@gmail.com).

## What information we collect

This website has no accounts, cart or checkout. When you order or enquire, you share information with us by message:

- **Name**, to address your order.
- **Phone number** (your WhatsApp number) and, if you choose, your email, to confirm your order and arrange delivery.
- **Delivery address**, to ship your order.
- **Order details**, meaning the soaps you ask for, the quantity and the total.

We do not collect card details. Payments are made by UPI directly to us, and we do not see or store your bank credentials.

## How we use your information

We use it only to confirm and make your order, to contact you about its status or delivery, and to handle any question or return. We do not use your personal information for marketing without your consent, and we never sell or rent your data.

## Analytics and cookies

We use these tools to understand how visitors use the website and how our advertising performs:

- **Google Analytics 4** collects anonymised data about page visits, traffic sources and general browsing. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Microsoft Clarity** records anonymised heatmaps and session replays to show how visitors move through the pages. Personal details are masked automatically. See [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).
- **Meta Pixel** helps us measure advertising on Facebook and Instagram. You can manage your preferences in [Meta's Privacy Centre](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/explanation).

These tools may place cookies in your browser. You can manage or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

## Data retention

Order information (name, phone number, address and order details) is kept for three years to meet accounting and tax obligations under Indian law. Analytics data is kept according to each tool's default settings.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at [healingsoil.in@gmail.com](mailto:healingsoil.in@gmail.com) and we will reply within a reasonable time.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a revised date at the top.
