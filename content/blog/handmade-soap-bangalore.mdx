Healing Soil makes soap in small batches in Goa and ships across India, including Bangalore. If you are choosing your first bar, start with the base: it has more influence on lather and after-wash feel than the colour or botanical on the label.

## Choose the soap base first

- **Glycerin** gives a light lather and an easy-rinsing feel.
- **Goat milk** gives a creamier lather and a nourishing feel.
- **Shea butter** is the richest, most conditioning base in the range.

All Healing Soil bars are made without SLS, parabens, or synthetic fragrance. Neem and tulsi used in selected bars are grown on our farm; other ingredients are identified as sourced.

Read our [glycerin and goat milk soap comparison](/blog/glycerin-vs-goat-milk-soap) if you want a side-by-side guide.

## Soap and Bangalore water

Water hardness varies across Bangalore by neighbourhood and water source. In harder water, soap may take longer to lather. Wet the bar and your hands, build the lather gradually, and rinse well rather than using a large amount at once.

## Keep handmade soap dry

A draining soap dish matters in Bangalore's humid and rainy months. Let water move away from the bar, avoid keeping it under the shower stream, and allow it to dry between uses. Store unopened bars in a cool, dry cupboard.
