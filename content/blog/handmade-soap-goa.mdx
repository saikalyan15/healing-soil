Healing Soil soap is melted, hand-poured, and packed in small batches in Goa. Each bar is made without SLS, parabens, or synthetic fragrance.

## What is made here and what is sourced

We source the glycerin, goat milk, and shea butter soap bases from a specialist manufacturer. At the farm, we melt the chosen base, prepare the botanicals, pour the bars by hand, and let them set before packing.

Neem and tulsi described as farm-grown are grown on our property in Goa, harvested, dried, and prepared here. Other ingredients are sourced and named clearly on the product page.

## Three soap bases, three textures

- [Glycerin soap](/glycerin-soap) has a light lather and rinses easily.
- [Goat milk soap](/goat-milk-soap) has a creamy texture and nourishing feel.
- [Shea butter soap](/shea-butter-soap) is the richest and most conditioning option.

The best starting point is the texture you already enjoy. Our [soap-base comparison](/blog/glycerin-vs-goat-milk-soap) explains the differences in more detail.

## Made to order

Small batches let us prepare bars close to the time they are ordered. Dispatch and delivery estimates are shown during checkout. We ship across India from Goa.

## Care after delivery

Keep the bar on a draining soap dish and let it dry between uses. Store spare bars in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and bathroom humidity.
